Agentic systems

A personal AI assistant that’s always available

Operational core

I run a Hermes assistant on a dedicated, always-on Mac mini and interact with it in natural language through iMessage. I wanted an assistant I could reach from a tool I already use and return to as work continues.

I’m exploring scheduled tasks, proactive notifications, and how an assistant could coordinate work across services with human review. A similar setup could support recurring follow-ups for small teams or help individuals stay on top of ongoing responsibilities.

Implementation evidence

Space for redacted interactions and a summary of verified integrations and workflows.

Research & exploration

Intelligent product discovery

Finding sealed Pokémon TCG products at reasonable prices takes repeated searches across retailers. I’m exploring a monitoring workflow that filters relevant listings, checks stock, compares prices with MSRP and configurable thresholds, and sends useful iMessage alerts.

The same approach could help teams monitor suppliers, inventory, and pricing. The monitoring and alerting are proposed capabilities.

Research & exploration

Smart home intelligence

Connected devices produce separate streams of events. I’m exploring how a persistent assistant could interpret them together, surface important events, and summarize activity in a way that helps someone decide what needs attention.

Footage review, automated summaries, and context-aware alerts are research directions.

My approach

How I build with AI

Start with the problem

Understand the challenge and the people affected before choosing a tool.

Find meaningful opportunities

Look for repetitive work and unnecessary steps that AI could help simplify.

Build for everyday use

Choose solutions people can use, maintain, and adapt as their work changes.

Keep humans involved

Build in review when a system influences important decisions or acts on someone’s behalf.

Make AI accessible

Use plain language and hands-on examples so people can find and try their own use cases.

Iterate and improve

Observe what works, learn from mistakes, and refine the workflow over time.

Bringing practical AI to more teams

I’d like to bring what I’ve learned from internal workshops to small businesses, nonprofits, and individuals. I would begin by listening to how they work and finding one place where AI could ease a bottleneck.

From there, I’d build something suited to their needs, teach them how to use and review it, and help them develop the confidence to maintain it independently.
